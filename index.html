Coffee, not beer

The storied corner of W45th Street and 10th Avenue has entered a new chapter. Where the neighborhood once raised pints, it now raises mugs — Coffee T and Bagels replaced the old bar stools with espresso machines, fresh bagels and morning tables.

We're officially open and pouring from morning to night. Stop by, say hi, and see what all of Hell's Kitchen is talking about.
